# Iskar discharge pack provenance

Generated by `scripts/build_iskar_pack.py` from the EIT Water Hackathon organiser files in `Share/`:

- `Искър_Нови_Искър_2025.csv`: daily discharge (m³/s), Iskar at Novi Iskar.
- `Искър_Ореховица_2025.csv`: daily discharge (m³/s), Iskar at Orehovitsa.

Window: 2025-07-12 to 2025-12-31, 146 days per station, with gaps (Orehovitsa has no values 4–20 Oct 2025).
Provenance: **supplied_dataset**, hackathon use. Missing days stay missing.

These records are native 2025. The Danube pack is 2021, so the two do not overlap natively. The platform shows
Iskar only as a **scenario-aligned** tributary input on the common 2025 scenario timeline (P0-19 v1.3 §4.7, §8.7):
no Iskar–Danube correlation is computed, and no pollutant travel time or Danube-level impact is inferred.

Station coordinates are not in the source files; `stations.csv` holds approximate positions (`coords_approx=true`).
