# Danube level pack provenance

Generated by `scripts/build_danube_pack.py` from the EIT Water Hackathon organiser files in `Share/Danube`:

- `БюлетинЗаХидрологичнатаОбстановкаПоРекаДунав.csv`: daily hydrological bulletin, water level (cm) for
  51 Danube stations (Schwabelweis km 2376 to Tulcea km 71) and 9 tributary stations (Tisza, Sava, Velika Morava).
- `АвтоматичниXидрометричниСтанции.csv`: Bulgarian automatic gauges (Novo Selo km 834 to Malak Preslavets km 414),
  water level (cm) and water temperature (°C).

Window: 2021-06-06 to 2021-11-09, one value per station per day. Provenance: **organiser**, hackathon use.

Cleaning: date separator rows, "притоци" header rows and duplicate (date, station) rows are dropped;
`-987` and `nil` are treated as missing. Values are otherwise as published.

Station coordinates are **not** in the source files. `stations.csv` holds approximate town or bridge positions
(`coords_approx=true`), good enough for a map marker and the nearest-station lookup, not for survey work.
